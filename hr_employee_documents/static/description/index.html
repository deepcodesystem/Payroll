📄 Employee Document Management

Version: 18.0.1.0.0 | Category: Human Resources / Payroll

Overview

Comprehensive solution for managing and distributing employee documents. Allow HR managers and responsible persons to upload, manage, approve, and control access to various employee documents such as work certificates, training certificates, and more.

✨ Key Features

📤 Document Upload
Upload various types of employee documents (PDF, Word, etc.) with automatic metadata tracking.
🏷️ Document Organization
Categorize documents using predefined types: Work Certificates, Training Certificates, Salary Certificates, Medical Records, Performance Reviews, and more.
✅ Approval Workflow
Complete approval workflow with Draft, Pending, Approved, and Rejected statuses with detailed audit trail.
🔐 Access Control
Role-based permissions with manager assignment, visibility controls, and approval requirements by document type.
🔍 Advanced Search & Filtering
Search by employee, type, dates, status. Group by employee, type, status, or manager. Quick filters for expiry tracking.
📅 Expiry Tracking
Track document expiration dates and identify expired documents at a glance.
📊 Reports & Analytics
Generate PDF reports of employee documents with status and details.
📧 Notifications
Automatic notifications to employees about document status changes through chatter.

👥 User Roles & Permissions

  • HR Managers: Full access to create, edit, approve, and delete documents
  • HR Officers: Full access to manage documents
  • Managers: Can view and manage subordinate documents
  • Employees: Can view approved documents visible to them

🔧 Integration

Seamlessly integrates with:

  • HR Module (Employees, Contracts, Attendance)
  • Payroll Module
  • Email & Notifications (Chatter)

📝 How to Use

For HR Managers

  1. Go to Human Resources > Employee Documents > Documents
  2. Click Create to add a new document
  3. Select employee, document type, and upload file
  4. Save and manage the document lifecycle
  5. Approve or reject pending documents

For Employees

  1. Go to Human Resources > Employees > Your Profile
  2. Select the Documents tab
  3. View approved documents
  4. Download documents as needed
